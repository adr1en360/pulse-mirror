# Surface brief: Pulse Mirror main window

Mode: Experience. The face leads; the interface recedes.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A home-movie projection of your own heartbeat. The one idea: the wait to find your pulse is an old film-leader countdown, and the pulse, once found, is the only thing that moves. It refuses the category default of a dark health-app panel with graphs and number tiles.

OWN-WORLD: Academy-leader grey field with white hairline crosshair and concentric circles over a near-black stage. White condensed numerals as the only large type; a quiet humanist sans for sentences. One signal colour, vermilion, reserved for the live pulse and nothing else. Grain stays still. No flicker, no glow. Controls are plain labelled keys. The doodle heart looks scratched into film by hand.

STORY: A curious person understands within seconds that the camera is looking for their heartbeat, trusts it because the camera-on mark and consent are plain, watches the countdown arm stop and their own face begin to beat, and captures a six-second loop to send to someone.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed stage, the live mirror centred at 16:9, no side panel. Over it the leader: crosshair circle, a sweeping arm, a large numeral at centre. A slim bottom strip holds Start/Stop, Capture, and a single status sentence. The camera-on mark sits top left.

FORM: Academy film leader and Super 8 home-movie ritual, candidate 6 on the ordered list. seed:fb04deba21631e85

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Scope and decisions

Audience: one curious person at a laptop, about a minute, lamp light. Success: "that is my heart", then share a clip.
Build path: code-led (app screen).
Pinned by the user: a hand-drawn-style animated figure appears in the shared clip.
Constraints from the design taste check: no pure black, no neon glow, one accent, a non-default type pairing served from local files, empty/loading/error states present, tactile feedback on press.
Unresolved: exact numerals face; reduced-motion treatment of the throb.
