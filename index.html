Your heartbeat, in your own face

Pulse Mirror uses your camera to find your pulse, then shows it in your reflection.

Video is processed on this computer and is never saved or sent. While it runs, the Presage SDK checks your access key online and sends numbers derived from your pulse and breathing (never video or images) to Presage, which passes them to a language model; this app does not show that output. Telemetry is off. Nothing is stored unless you save a clip. For curiosity only, not a medical device. Sit still and face a lamp or a window.
